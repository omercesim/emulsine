Emulsine

Privacy policy

Effective 7 September 2026.

Emulsine has no user accounts, advertising or analytics. Photo and depth processing runs on your device. We do not operate a service that receives your photographs or app usage data.

Camera and Photos

The app requests Camera access to take photographs and Photos access to save and display photographs. Files and development information are stored locally. If you enable iCloud Photos or device backups, Apple may sync that data according to your settings. Sharing or exporting a photograph sends it only to the destination you choose.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases. Emulsine uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to unlock purchased features; it does not receive your payment-card details. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Apple's standard licence agreement applies.

Settings and diagnostics

Preferences, custom presets, imported LUTs and diagnostic logs are stored locally. Logs are not uploaded automatically. If you email a support request or attach a log or photograph, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to that request.

This website

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to operate and secure the site, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. This does not upload photographs or usage data from the Emulsine app.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published here with a new effective date. For privacy or support questions, email omercesim@gmail.com.
