Support

A film emulation camera for iPhone, with control over color, texture and depth.

Questions and problems

Contact info@omercesim.art. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description. You can voluntarily attach the local log.txt file from Files → On My iPhone → Emulsine to help diagnose a problem.

Emulsine Pro

KP 400, FP 400H and IH 5, 35mm grain, medium halation and low diffusion are included. Pro adds the full film library, custom presets and LUTs, additional effects, portrait styles and RAW re-development. Open Settings → Emulsine Pro to purchase or restore access. A free, non-renewing 7-day Trial opens all Pro features before you choose a monthly, yearly or lifetime purchase. After the trial, the free camera remains available. Eligible monthly and yearly subscribers may also receive an introductory trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled; manage them in your Apple Account settings.

Your photographs

Camera and Photos permissions are required to take and save photographs. RAW formats and resolution depend on your iPhone and selected lens. Keep the RAW negative to develop a frame again later.
